Enter your loan details and select Calculate EMI to see your monthly payment and breakdown.

How to use

Some education loans have a moratorium period; this tool assumes repayment starts immediately. Uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula; actual bank figures may differ.

FAQ

Is this the exact bank EMI?

It is an estimate; fees and rounding can change it.

Does a longer tenure save money?

It lowers the EMI but raises total interest.
