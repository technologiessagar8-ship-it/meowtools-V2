How to use

AVIF is a modern high-compression format; support depends on your browser. JPG is a small, widely supported photo format that uses lossy compression. Select your AVIF files and download the JPG versions.

FAQ

Does AVIF to JPG lose quality?

Converting to JPG is lossy but quality stays high. Transparency is kept only when the output format supports it.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens locally in your browser.
