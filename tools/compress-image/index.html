Compress Image to KB

Reduce a JPG, PNG or WebP photo to your target size in KB, right in your browser.

✦ Browser-based♧ Private by design⚡ Free to use
▧

How to use

Useful for online forms that reject large photos. The tool lowers JPEG quality, then scales the image down only if needed.

FAQ

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Compression runs in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Why is the output JPG?

JPG gives the best size control. Transparent areas turn white.
