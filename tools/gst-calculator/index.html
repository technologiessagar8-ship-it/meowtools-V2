GST Calculator (Add or Remove GST)

Add GST to a price or find the price before GST.

How to use

Enter the amount and GST rate. For intra-state sales, GST splits equally into CGST and SGST.

FAQ

Which rate should I use?

Use the rate applicable to your product or service.

No, it only does arithmetic.
