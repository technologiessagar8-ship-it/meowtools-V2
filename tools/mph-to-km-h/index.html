How to use

To convert mph to km/h, multiply by 1.60934. Quick reference: 1 mph = 1.60934 km/h; 5 mph = 8.04672 km/h; 10 mph = 16.0934 km/h; 50 mph = 80.4672 km/h; 100 mph = 160.934 km/h.

FAQ

How many km/h are in 1 mph?

1 mph equals 1.60934 km/h.

How do I convert km/h to mph?
