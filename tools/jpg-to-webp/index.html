How to use

JPG is a small, widely supported photo format that uses lossy compression. WebP is a modern format that is usually smaller than JPG or PNG. Select your JPG files and download the WebP versions.

FAQ

Does JPG to WebP lose quality?

Converting to WebP keeps the visible quality. Transparency is kept only when the output format supports it.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens locally in your browser.
