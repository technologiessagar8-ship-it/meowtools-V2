How to use

BMP is an uncompressed format with large files. JPG is a small, widely supported photo format that uses lossy compression. Select your BMP files and download the JPG versions.

FAQ

Does BMP to JPG lose quality?

Converting to JPG is lossy but quality stays high. Transparency is kept only when the output format supports it.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens locally in your browser.
