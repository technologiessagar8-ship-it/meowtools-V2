Loan EMI Calculator

Work out the monthly EMI, total interest and total payment of a loan.

How to use

EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where r is the monthly rate and n the number of months. Real bank EMIs may differ slightly due to fees.

FAQ

Is this the exact bank EMI?

It is an estimate using the standard formula.

Does it include processing fees?

No.
