Home Loan EMI Calculator

Estimate the monthly EMI, total interest and total cost of a home loan.

How to use

Home loans have long tenures, so a small rate change shifts total interest a lot. Uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula; actual bank figures may differ.

FAQ

Is this the exact bank EMI?

It is an estimate; fees and rounding can change it.

Does a longer tenure save money?

It lowers the EMI but raises total interest.
