USE THE RIGHT INPUTS

Use regional settings and units with care

  • VAT rules, standard rates, reduced rates and exemptions differ by country and can change. This calculator uses your input; verify current official guidance before filing or billing.
  • The volume conversion listed as US gallons uses US liquid gallons, not Imperial gallons. Choose a converter whose units match your exact use case.
  • MeowTools offers a shared English-language experience worldwide. Regional pages help visitors find relevant tools; the tools do not automatically determine legal, tax or financial rules for each country.
COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does the VAT calculator apply the right UK or EU rate automatically?

No. It calculates using the rate you enter. Check the current rules and rate for the relevant country and transaction.

Are US and Imperial gallons the same?

No. This site’s gallons converter is labeled US gallons to liters; do not treat it as an Imperial-gallon conversion.

Are the tools translated into every European language?

The current toolkit is primarily in English. These regional guides organize useful tools but are not full translations of every page.
