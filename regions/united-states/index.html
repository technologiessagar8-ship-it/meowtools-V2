COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does the sales-tax calculator pick my local rate?

No. It uses a rate you provide; confirm the current rate and tax treatment for your location and transaction.

Is the gallon converter based on US gallons?

Yes. The tool is specifically labeled US gallons to liters and should not be used as an Imperial-gallon converter.

Can I use these tools on a phone?

Yes. The pages are designed to adapt to phone, tablet and desktop widths; complex file tasks may still be easier on a larger screen.
